# Changelog

## 1.2.0: Stance Overhaul support

With [Fontaine's Stance Overhaul](https://sp-mod.com/mod/3136/spt-stance-overhaul) installed, opening and closing the attachment menu follows your current stance, matching its movement and timing.

**Fully optional, but recommended for smoother animations. All Field Attachments features work without it.**

Supports **Stance Overhaul 0.0.2** with **[Realism Common Library 0.0.2](https://sp-mod.com/mod/3135/spt-realism-common-lib)**. A dismissible notice on the main menu shows whether stance support is ready.

### Also in this update

- Preview ergonomics, vertical and horizontal recoil, and weight before fitting a part. The full comparison appears at the top right, with smaller ergonomics and recoil bars beside the list.
- Add a Multitool layer for stocks, handguards and pistol grips. Carry a Leatherman Multitool to change these parts. Required parts need a compatible replacement and cannot be removed on their own.
- Use the same in-game fitting and removal animations for basic and advanced parts, with quicker entry into the attachment view and an optional faster close.
- Open the parts list by clicking its card's thumbnail. Remove the duplicate LIST button and give REMOVE the full width beneath the thumbnail.
- Add an Unavailable list explaining why a carried part cannot be used at the selected mounting point.
- Keep Left Alt with Hold as the menu default, set middle mouse to switch Basic / Multitool layers, and use the wheel to browse the open list. Scrolling never installs a part. Menu shortcuts support keyboard keys and mouse buttons with optional modifiers; updates preserve saved bindings.
- Add adjustable idle breathing movement that settles while choosing a part and pauses during clicks.
- Refresh the header, bottom control hints and layer switch feedback while preserving the tilted cards.
- Fix overlapping cards so the part you preview is the one your click selects. Disabled controls no longer let clicks through to another card. Improve how the weapon returns when you close or interrupt the menu.
- Keep the existing 996-weapon coverage and optional weapon packs.

## 1.1.0 — Optional weapon pack support

- Expand support to 996 weapon entries: 159 standard SPT entries and 837 from seven optional packs.
- Add Definitive Weapon Variants, WTT Armory, C11 True North and Make Shotguns Great Again, alongside updated Backport, Epic's All in One and ECOT support.
- Keep all weapon packs optional. Standard SPT weapons work without them; install the packs you want separately.
- Preserve the existing cards, controls, animation timing and guarded attachment changes.

## 1.0.0

- Angled attachment cards for optics, muzzle devices, tactical devices and foregrips.
- Left Alt opens the menu and cursor, with Hold or Toggle available in F12.
- Install, replace or remove compatible carried parts, and cycle mounting positions with Alt+R.
- Attachment changes are enabled by default on fresh installs. Updates preserve saved settings; disabling changes leaves browsing available.
- Custom attachment animations are always enabled. The old O shortcut is disabled by default.
- Includes the settings cleanup and weapon support from 0.25.4.

## 0.25.4 — Simpler settings

- Make Left Alt the only menu shortcut enabled by default.
- Keep custom attachment animations always on and remove their settings toggle.

## 0.25.3 — Alt menu controls

- Hold Left Alt to open the existing attachment cards with cursor control; release to close.
- Choose Hold or Toggle in F12. Toggle keeps Alt+R available for mounting positions.
- Add a short bottom control hint. Keep the original 0.25 card layout, animation timing and attachment operations.

## 0.25.2 — Presentation baseline

Improve the hand position on pistols and add support for more weapons, including the RShG-2 and flares.

## 0.25.0 — Standalone presentation

Give attachment mode its own weapon presentation, separate from normal weapon inspection.
